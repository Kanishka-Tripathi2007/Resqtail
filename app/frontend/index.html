🏠 Rescue & Adoption

Save a life. Give a home.

Learn how to safely rescue an injured street animal, or open your heart and home to one looking for a family.

01

Assess the Situation

Stay calm. Don't rush toward the animal. Assess injury level — is it conscious, breathing, bleeding severely? Note the location details.

02

Call for Professional Help

Call the nearest shelter or NGO BEFORE attempting to move the animal. Describe the injury and location clearly. They will guide you.

03

Provide Immediate Safety

If safe to do so, keep the animal away from traffic. Use a cardboard box or soft cloth. Avoid sudden movements that may startle it.

04

Safe Transport

Use a box or blanket as a makeshift stretcher. Support the full body. Keep the animal warm and dark. Drive smoothly and slowly to the vet.

05

Hand Over to Professionals

Provide all information to the vet — where you found it, estimated age, visible injuries. Follow up if possible to know its status.

❌ What NOT to Do

  • Don't pick up a severely injured animal without proper support — you may worsen fractures
  • Don't give any food, water or human medication without vet guidance
  • Don't hold the animal tightly — fear can cause biting or scratching
  • Don't leave a rescued animal unattended near a road
  • Don't delay calling a vet — time is critical for injured animals
  • Don't attempt to set broken bones or dress deep wounds yourself
